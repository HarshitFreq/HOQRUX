# PRIVACY POLICY // HOQRUX

**Last updated:** November 21, 2026  
**Effective date:** November 21, 2026  
**Application:** HOQRUX ("we", "our", or "the app")  

HOQRUX is a privacy-first, Swiss Brutalist cinema, television, and anime tracking application. We respect your digital sovereignty. This Privacy Policy discloses what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your rights regarding your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
* **Account Credentials (Optional):** If you register an account, we collect your email address and an optional display username. If you use HOQRUX as a guest without creating an account, no personal credentials are collected or transmitted.
* **User-Generated Tracking Content:** Titles you mark as watched, ratings, episode progression, and custom watchlists.

### B. Information Automatically Collected
* **Device & Diagnostic Data:** Basic platform data (operating system, app version) strictly used to ensure app performance and prevent crashes.
* **No Ad Tracking:** HOQRUX contains **zero third-party advertisements, zero ad trackers, and zero marketing telemetry SDKs**.

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Stored & Transferred

* **Local-First Storage:** All viewing logs, watchlists, and title ratings are stored locally on your device using encrypted, sandboxed storage (`AsyncStorage`).
* **Cloud Synchronization (For Registered Users):** When you sign in, your tracking data is synced to Supabase servers. All network transmissions are strictly encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (**HTTPS / TLS 1.3**).
* **Third-Party APIs:**
  * **The Movie Database (TMDb):** Used to fetch metadata, synopses, posters, cast, and provider availability. Queries to TMDb are non-identifying media lookups. *This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.*
  * **AniList & TVmaze:** Used for supplementary television and anime catalog data.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have complete control over your data at all times:
* **In-App Account Deletion:** You can permanently delete your account and purge all associated cloud data at any time from within the app:  
  `PROFILE` → `ABOUT & DATA` → `DELETE ACCOUNT & PURGE ALL DATA`.
* **Web-Based Data Deletion Request:** If you do not have the app installed, you can submit an account and data purge request via our public [Account & Data Deletion Portal](ACCOUNT_DELETION.md) or by emailing us at `privacy@hoqrux.app`. Upon verification, all records are permanently erased within 48 hours.
* **Local Data Erase:** Guest users can clear all local logs with one tap under `PROFILE` → `ABOUT & DATA` → `PURGE ALL LOCAL DEVICE DATA`.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

HOQRUX does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for immediate deletion.
